Yourself First — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

What this extension does

Yourself First helps you pause easy lookups and everyday decisions before they are sent to ChatGPT, and can hide Google’s AI Overview on search result pages.

Data we process

  • ChatGPT: The extension reads the text you are about to send so it can classify that prompt on your device. Prompt text is not stored and is not sent to our servers.
  • Google search: The extension inspects page structure only as needed to hide the AI Overview block. It does not save your search queries.
  • Stored on your device (and via Chrome Sync when signed in): aggregate statistics (prompts held, overridden, estimated tokens kept), settings, pause state, and a local device id.

Network

Classification runs locally. The extension does not call a Yourself First backend. If you use “Search the web instead”, Chrome opens a normal Google search tab for your query.

Permissions

storage — settings, pause state, and statistics. Content scripts run on ChatGPT to intercept sends, and on Google search pages to hide AI Overview.

Contact

For privacy questions about Yourself First, contact the publisher listed on the Chrome Web Store listing for this extension.
